 {{ heading('I Have A Budget', 'IHAB · Python + SQLite prototype 0.2') }}

A place to categorize spending, review your habits, and plan each month.

This app runs on your computer. Python handles the pages, budget calculations, storage, and Plaid integration. A small browser script handles controls and Plaid Link.

Shared family logins, investments imports, splits, recurring bills, and the full credit-card payment budget model are planned.

Balances are imported snapshots. Category planning conservatively reserves card debt; reconcile against your accounts before relying on allocations.

Demo, Sandbox, and production data are isolated. Sandbox connections contain test data.
